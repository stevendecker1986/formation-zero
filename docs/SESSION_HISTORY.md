# Personal session history

History is private and actor-scoped at every query. It lists completed or abandoned sessions and supports bounded date, objective, completion-state, and result-limit filters. Session detail retains the prescribed snapshot, terminal timestamps, factual elapsed duration, append-only actual revisions, skips, and authorized substitutions.

The history is factual. It does not score readiness, calculate training load, forecast results, adapt programming, rank users, or expose records to leaders, coaches, formations, or other accounts.
