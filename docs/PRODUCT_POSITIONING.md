# Formation Zero — universal human performance

Formation Zero is a universal fitness, human-performance, recovery, readiness, and group-training platform. **Readiness Starts Here.** Military affiliation is never a prerequisite for receiving full product value. The current implementation is the Phase A account/platform foundation; this positioning does not imply future fitness features are available.

The audience includes beginners, recreational and strength athletes, muscle-development users, runners, hybrid athletes, ruckers, tactical athletes, military personnel, veterans, law enforcement, firefighters, first responders, trainers, coaches, teams, academies, ROTC, group instructors, tactical-performance organizations, people preparing for demanding occupations and organizations responsible for group training. Use user, athlete, individual, member, participant, leader, coach, trainer, team, group or formation as appropriate. Never assume a default user is a Marine.

## Accounts and later onboarding

Accounts must not require military service, branch, rank, MOS, unit, billet, service/veteran status, DoD/government email or government affiliation. No such field is added. Future optional affiliation requires a separately approved legitimate product requirement.

Later onboarding should support general fitness, strength, muscle development, running, hybrid performance, rucking, tactical fitness, work capacity, mobility, recovery, body-composition support, return to structured training, event preparation and custom goals. PFT/CFT are specialized modules; they must not dominate general onboarding or impose irrelevant questions on civilians. No onboarding is implemented here.

## COMMAND and tiers

COMMAND serves universal group training: people, time, equipment, space, ability levels, restrictions, recent training history and objective inform a future executable group session. Users may be coaches, teams, trainers, fire/law-enforcement organizations, academies, ROTC, military leaders or any group-training organization.

Future formation / parent_formation / formation_type must support flexible hierarchy. Potential types include CUSTOM_GROUP, TEAM, CLASS, CREW, SECTION, FIRE_TEAM, SQUAD, PLATOON, COMPANY, ORGANIZATION and OTHER. These examples are not implemented types or mandatory military structures.

Exactly BASE, PERFORMANCE and COMMAND remain. BASE has none of the nine premium flags; its future free functionality requires separate specification. PERFORMANCE retains CAN_USE_FULL_LIBRARY, CAN_USE_ADAPTIVE_PROGRAMMING, CAN_USE_READINESS_ENGINE, CAN_USE_ADVANCED_PROGRESS, CAN_USE_RUN_TRACKING and CAN_USE_RUCK_TRACKING. All tiers receive the same visual quality. No capability, billing or commercial gate is activated.
