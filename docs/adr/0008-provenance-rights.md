# 0008 — Content foundations without a CMS

Status: Accepted for Phase A. Date: 2026-09-04.

## Context

Content is structured/versioned data in future phases, and UNKNOWN rights must not publish.

## Decision

Implement only exact status/provenance/rights enums, runtime validators and conservative necessary-condition helper requiring approved/published status, verified evidence and a publishable rights category.

## Consequences

No content tables, ingestion, copied policy/media, approval workflow or publish endpoint. Helper is not legal authorization. No future fitness or Marine Corps doctrine is invented.
