# Volume, intensity and rest

Complete reviewed dose options are objective-specific, versioned with their exercise/recovery content and limited to eight per candidate. There are no production presets. Fixtures are software numbers, not professional training advice.

REPS: sets × reps × seconds_per_rep; between-set rest is (sets − 1) × rest_seconds. TIME: explicit continuous work seconds. DISTANCE: explicit meters and estimated work seconds supplied by the profile, without pace calculation. INTERVALS: rounds × work_seconds and (rounds − 1) × rest_seconds. Setup/transition seconds are always added. Volume primitives provide total target repetitions, distance, interval/round counts and work/rest totals without inventing loads. Individual conditioning supports continuous and interval structures, not group stations.

Representational bounds: sets/reps/rounds 1–100, seconds 0–14400, meters 1–100000, seconds_per_rep 1–60. These are schema/resource bounds, not medical limits. Required clinical/technical limits belong to reviewed profiles and Phase C. Rest cannot fall below minimum_rest_seconds; the engine never rewrites a dose to squeeze it into available time.

Intensity includes level 0–5, EASY/MODERATE/HARD, BODYWEIGHT, RPE (0–10), PERCENTAGE (0–100), PACE_ZONE, LOAD and TIME_DISTANCE. PACE_ZONE/LOAD require externally supplied values; PERCENTAGE requires a supplied 1RM reference and preserves the percentage without calculating 1RM or absolute weight. No pace zones, readiness, thresholds or training loads are calculated.

The reviewed candidate intensity is a known minimum floor: dose levels cannot undercut it. Every proposed dose intensity is re-evaluated through Phase C; a lower dose never resurrects a blocked base candidate. Supplied readiness/load/phase effects are exclusively Phase C rules.
